Sea Chefs is one of the world's leading cruise ship hotel management companies, headquartered in Frankfurt, Germany. As a subsidiary of the Marlow Group, we provide comprehensive hotel operations — culinary, restaurant service, housekeeping, and guest relations — across major international cruise lines.

We act as the bridge between top international hospitality talent and the world's premier cruise brands, including TUI Cruises Mein Schiff, Hapag-Lloyd Cruises, and Phoenix Reisen. Our mission is simple: deliver exceptional guest experiences at sea through professional, motivated, and well-trained crew.

What Is Expected
Onboard Standards
01
Uniform & Appearance
Full uniform must be worn at all times during working hours — clean, pressed, name badge visible. Hair neatly groomed. No strong perfume or jewellery beyond company policy.
02
Guest Service Standard
Every guest interaction must reflect warmth, professionalism, and attentiveness. The 10-5 rule applies: smile at 10 feet, greet at 5 feet. Complaints are opportunities to excel.
03
Food Safety & Hygiene (HACCP)
Strict HACCP protocols at all times. Handwashing frequency, temperature monitoring, allergen awareness, and cross-contamination prevention are non-negotiable.
04
Safety at Sea (STCW)
All crew must hold valid STCW certifications. Participation in muster drills is mandatory. All crew must know their station, lifeboat procedures, and fire response protocols.
05
Communication & Reporting
All incidents — safety, service, or personnel — must be reported through the correct chain of command immediately. Clear, honest communication is a core professional duty.
Life at Sea
Professional Conduct
Zero Tolerance
Alcohol & Substances
Alcohol and substance use during duty hours is strictly prohibited with zero exceptions. Off-duty consumption is subject to ship regulations and port-specific rules.
Required
Cabin Sharing
Shared cabins are standard. Respect for shared space, noise curfews (22:00–07:00), and personal boundaries is a condition of employment, not a request.
Policy
Social Media
Off-duty social media use is permitted but must never disclose guest information, vessel locations, internal operations, or any confidential company information.
Good to Know
Contract Duration
Contracts typically run 4–9 months depending on position and vessel. Extensions are based on performance review and operational requirements at end of contract.
