Who They Are
Brand Concept

TUI Cruises is a 50:50 joint venture between TUI AG (Germany) and Royal Caribbean Cruises Ltd., founded in 2008 and headquartered in Hamburg. The company launched operations in 2009 and rapidly grew into one of Europe's leading premium cruise brands.

The Mein Schiff ("My Ship") brand is built around a single philosophy: Wohlfühlen — German for "well-being." Every ship is designed to feel like a floating resort, combining generous space, modern design, and a relaxed atmosphere. The primary language onboard is German, spoken by crew and used in all menus, announcements, and programmes.

TUI Cruises operates in the premium volume segment — filling the gap between a classic cruise and a club cruise. It is not a party ship, nor a luxury expedition — it is refined, modern, and guest-centred.

What Crew Must Know
Brand Standards
01
Language Onboard is German
All menus, daily programmes, and PA announcements are in German. Crew must speak English for internal communication and safety, but service interactions with guests are in German. Non-German crew must know key service phrases.
02
PAI — Premium All-Inclusive Explained
The PAI (Premium Alles Inklusive) concept is the core of the Mein Schiff brand. Most meals, 100+ drinks, and gratuities are included by default. Crew must clearly understand what is included vs. what carries a surcharge to avoid guest confusion.
03
Wohlfühlen — The Feel-Good Philosophy
The Mein Schiff brand promise is well-being. Service must feel warm, relaxed, and attentive — never stiff or transactional. Guests expect a personal, resort-style experience. High pressure or rushed service is off-brand.
04
Open Seating — No Fixed Dining Times
Unlike traditional cruise lines, Mein Schiff uses open seating in main restaurants. Guests can dine at their preferred time without a reservation. This means higher flexibility for guests but requires crew to manage flow and wait times professionally.
05
Sustainability & Environmental Awareness
The Mein Schiff fleet is one of the world's youngest and most eco-efficient. Newer ships use LNG fuel and zero-emission shore power. Crew should be aware of onboard waste management procedures and the brand's environmental commitments.
